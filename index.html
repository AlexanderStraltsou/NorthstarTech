I am Alex, Software Developer and Owner of Northstar Tech

About Me

Getting my first stationary PC opened up a whole new world for me. I was endlessly fascinated by taking off the side panel, exploring the components and understanding how each part fit together to make the machine run. Building software and websites gives me that exact same feeling today, assembling complex, working systems out of fundamental parts.

The turning point came when a friend needed a website for their shop and I decided to try building it myself for the very first time. Diving headfirst into HTML and CSS to build something real transformed web technology from an everyday tool into a hands-on craft I was determined to master.

One of my goals is to continuously grow Northstar Tech into a solid development company, delivering end-to-end full-stack solutions from an idea to final deployment. I want to achieve this because I enjoy turning ideas into practical, working solutions.
